# Controls

## In a match

|                    | Player 1 keyboard | Player 2 keyboard | Gamepad             |
| ------------------ | ----------------- | ----------------- | ------------------- |
| Move forward, back | `W` `S`           | `O` `L`           | Left stick or D-pad |
| Move left, right   | `A` `D`           | `K` `;`           | Left stick or D-pad |
| Left slap          | `C`               | `←`               | `LT`                |
| Right slap         | `V`               | `→`               | `RT`                |
| Double slap        | `C` and `V` together | `←` and `→` together | `LT` and `RT` together |
| Ability            | `E`               | `P`               | `X`                 |
| Pause              | `Esc`             | `Esc`             | `Start`             |
| Next round, after a win | `Space`      | `Space`           | `A`                 |

Gamepad buttons are named as on an Xbox controller. `Start` is the menu button on the right of the guide button.

## Ability select

|                          | Player 1 keyboard | Player 2 keyboard | Gamepad             |
| ------------------------ | ----------------- | ----------------- | ------------------- |
| Choose an ability        | `A` `D`           | `K` `;`           | Left stick or D-pad |
| Lock it in               | `C` or `V`        | `←` or `→`        | `X`                 |
| Change your mind         | `Esc`             | `Backspace`       | `B`                 |
| Back to the main menu (nobody locked in) | `Esc` | `Esc`        | `B`                 |
| Input setup (Local Multiplayer only) | `Shift` | `Shift`      | `Select`            |

## Menus

|                       | Keyboard                                     | Gamepad             |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Move through a menu   | `W` `A` `S` `D`, `O` `K` `L` `;` or the arrow keys | Left stick or D-pad |
| Change a setting      | Left and right on a volume, **Hitlag** or **Screen Shake** row | Left and right |
| Confirm               | `Space` or `Enter`                           | `A`                 |
| Back, close           | `Esc`                                        | `B`                 |

The pause menu has **Resume**, **Controls**, **Return to Ability Select** and **Return to Main Menu**. Press `Esc` or `Start` again to resume.

## Choosing your controls

By default, player 1 uses the left side of the keyboard (`W` `A` `S` `D`) and player 2 the right side (`O` `K` `L` `;`). There are two ways to change that:

- **Settings.** Select **P1 Input** or **P2 Input** and confirm, then press a key from the side you want (for example `W` or `O`) or any button on the gamepad you want. `Esc` or `B` cancels. The choice is saved.
- **Input setup.** On the ability select screen in **Local Multiplayer**, press `Shift` or `Select`. Each player then claims a device: press one of your keyboard side's movement or slap keys, or push a gamepad's stick or D-pad left for player 1 or right for player 2. Then ready up with `Shift` on the keyboard or `Select` on a gamepad.

With one gamepad as player 1 and nobody as player 2, pressing `Select` starts a solo game: the same gamepad also picks player 2's ability.
