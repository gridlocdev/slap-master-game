# How to play

Slap Master is a fighting game for two, inspired by Wii Sports Boxing and Circle of Sumo. You play a floating head with two big hands, and you win by slapping the other head off the arena.

## Game modes

- **Local Multiplayer**: two people on one computer, sharing the keyboard or using gamepads.
- **vs AI**: you against the computer. Pick **Easy**, **Medium**, **Hard** or **Expert** first. From **Hard** up, the computer watches how you play and adjusts to it over the rounds of a match.

## Rounds

A match is best of three: the first to win 2 rounds wins. Each round starts with a countdown (**3**, **2**, **1**, **TIME TO SLAP!!!**); you can slap during it but not move or use your ability. The score shows at the top of the screen.

When someone wins a round, press `Space` (or `A` on a gamepad) for the next round. After the match, the same button goes back to the main menu.

## Moving

You always face your opponent. Moving forward takes you toward them, moving back takes you away, and moving left or right circles round them.

## Slapping

- **Slap.** Each hand slaps on its own button. A slap that lands knocks your opponent back.
- **Double slap.** Press both slap buttons at the same time for a two-handed strike with much more knockback. It takes longer to wind up.
- **Clash.** If both players swing the same hand at the same moment, the slaps cancel and you both bounce apart.
- **Ring-out.** Knock your opponent off the edge of the arena to win the round.

## Abilities

On the ability select screen, each player picks one of three abilities, or **RANDOM**. You keep it for the whole match. The HUD shows each ability's cooldown, and **[READY]** when you can use it again.

- **Black Bean Burrito.** Your ability button dashes you forward, knocking your opponent back if you hit them, and leaves a stink cloud where you started. Anyone in a stink cloud moves at half speed. It recharges in 3 seconds.
- **Literal Child.** You're smaller and move twice as fast, but your slaps knock back half as far (full strength against another Literal Child). Your ability button is a taunt, usable once a second.
- **Melon.** Your ability button throws a melon forward. Slaps push it around, and a double slap next to it makes it explode and launch everyone nearby. It recharges in 3 seconds.

In **vs AI**, you pick your own ability and then the computer's.

## Settings

**Settings** on the main menu has:

- **Music Volume** and **SFX Volume**, in steps of 5%.
- **Hitlag**: the short freeze when a slap lands. On or off.
- **Screen Shake**: on or off.
- **P1 Input** and **P2 Input**: which controls each player uses. See [Controls](controls.md#choosing-your-controls).

Settings are saved in the browser and kept between visits.
